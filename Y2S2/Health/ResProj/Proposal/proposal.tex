\Section{Motivation and Research Question}
  
  How does having a child born with a risky health status impact migration decisions in new parents?

  Changes in family makeup can drive migration decisions for new parents. They may be seeking proximity to relatives, better opportunities to provide, or a more
  advantageous environment for their child to grow in. However, the goal of this idea is to examine how this decision is affected by having a child who is born
  with some sort of health risk, like being low birth weight (front of mind). Understanding the cases in which this can act as a motivator versus when it is a cost 
  can help to understand the general patterns of migration for an interesting group with multiple dynamics at play.
