\begin{frame}{Overview}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item How does influencer culture impact consumer beliefs in the vitamin and supplement market?
    \item How does consumer behavior change when exposed to influencer marketing?
    \item How does firm strategy adapt to the presence and impact of influencers?
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Key Contributions}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Use of experimental design to isolate the effect of influencer marketing on consumer demand.
    \item Policy implications of regulating influencer endorsements in the health supplement market.
    \item IV design leveraging exogenous variation in influencer reach via random experimental assignment.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Constructive Comments}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Consider exploring alternative IV strategies surrounding influencer reach, like using difference in how platform pushes something like TikTok Shop, which is a more covert form of advertising than traditional influencer posts. 
    \item Further discussion on potential confounders in the experimental design, such as pre-existing consumer attitudes towards supplements, i.e. cohort effects surrounding health trends.
    \item Explore heterogeneity in consumer responses based on demographics or prior health behaviors.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
